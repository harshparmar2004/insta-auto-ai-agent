Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Overview & Commitment

InstaAuto ("we," "our," or "the Platform") operates a creator engagement and workflow automation service enabling Instagram creators, businesses, and digital entrepreneurs to streamline post interactions and resource sharing.

We take the privacy and security of your personal information and your audience's data with utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy discloses what information we access, collect, process, and retain through our integration with the official Meta Graph API and Instagram Messaging Platform.

2. Information We Collect and Access

When you link your Instagram Professional account (Creator or Business) with InstaAuto via official Meta OAuth, we receive authorized access tokens to interact with your account:

  • Account Profile Data: Instagram User ID, username, public account name, and profile avatar URL to identify your account inside our dashboard.
  • Media Metadata: Public Reel and post IDs, captions, permalinks, and publication timestamps to allow you to configure keyword triggers.
  • Comment Webhook Events: Public comment text, comment IDs, commenter usernames, and user IDs posted on your media, used solely to evaluate configured automation triggers.
  • Direct Message Transmission Logs: Timestamp, recipient scoped ID, delivery status (pending, sent, failed), and tracking metrics for links dispatched via your automations.
Information We NEVER Collect or Store: We never collect, store, or have access to your Instagram account password, your private personal messages outside configured trigger contexts, payment card details, or sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We process data exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your creator session and displaying your content library in the dashboard.
  • Monitoring public comments in real time via official Meta Webhooks to detect your pre-defined trigger keywords.
  • Publishing public comment replies (e.g., "Check your DMs! 🚀") to inform commenters that their requested resource has been sent.
  • Sending automated direct messages containing your configured download links, discount codes, or resources within Meta's 24-hour messaging window.
  • Generating real-time analytics reports (click rates, delivery counts) visible only to you on your private dashboard.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any creator or follower data with any third party, data broker, or advertiser.

Data is communicated strictly between your browser, our secure application servers, and the official Meta Graph API servers in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard cryptographic practices:

  • All Meta Graph API access tokens are stored securely in isolated encrypted databases.
  • Tokens are automatically refreshed every 60 days via Meta's secure token refresh protocol and invalidated immediately upon account disconnection.
  • Webhook payloads are verified using HMAC SHA-256 signatures with our secret key to prevent spoofing or unauthorized injection.
  • All data in transit is protected using modern TLS/HTTPS encryption.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Instructions

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnecting Your Account: You can disconnect your Instagram account anytime from the Settings page in your InstaAuto dashboard, which immediately erases stored access tokens.
  • Revoking Permissions via Meta: You can revoke permissions directly inside your Facebook or Instagram settings under Settings & Privacy → Apps and Websites → InstaAuto → Remove.
  • Automated Data Deletion Callback: When you remove InstaAuto via Meta settings, our server receives Meta's automated signed request callback and permanently removes your stored tokens and associated webhook logs.
  • Manual Deletion Request: You can check your deletion status or submit a manual deletion request anytime via our Data Deletion Status Page or by emailing our privacy team.
