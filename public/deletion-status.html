How to Delete Your Data from InstaAuto:

  1. Via Meta / Facebook Settings: Go to your Facebook or Instagram Account → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → Locate InstaAuto → Click Remove. Meta will automatically trigger our Data Deletion Callback endpoint.
  2. Via InstaAuto Dashboard: Log in to your InstaAuto dashboard → Go to Settings → Click Disconnect Account to delete stored OAuth access tokens immediately.
  3. Manual Request: You may also email our data compliance team at support@instaauto.in with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and your Instagram username. All associated records will be permanently purged within 48 hours.
What happens upon deletion: All long-lived Instagram OAuth access tokens are permanently expunged, synced media caches are dropped, and comment webhook listeners for your account are decommissioned.
