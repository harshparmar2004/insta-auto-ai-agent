FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about InstaAuto AI Agent, Meta safety, and setup.

What is InstaAuto and how does it work?

+

InstaAuto is an autonomous Instagram automation SaaS that connects directly to the official Meta Graph API. It automatically detects follower comments on your Reels, verifies whether they follow your account, and instantly sends them a personalized direct message containing your link or download.

Is InstaAuto compliant with Meta's official Instagram terms?

+

Yes, 100%. Unlike risky web scrapers or browser bots that cause account bans, InstaAuto operates exclusively on the verified Meta Graph API Webhook & Messaging endpoints with humanized dispatch pacing (0.5s - 1.5s delay), guaranteeing full compliance.

How is InstaAuto different from ManyChat?

+

While ManyChat requires hours of setting up complicated drag-and-drop flowcharts and charges escalating contact fees, InstaAuto is specifically engineered for Reels creators with 24/7 autonomous monitoring, smart follower verification gates, and straightforward multi-tenant quotas.

Do I need to keep my laptop open or run anything locally?

+

Not at all! InstaAuto runs entirely in the cloud. Once your account is connected and your keywords are set, the AI Agent works 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, even while you are asleep or travelling.

What happens if I reach my hourly rate limit?

+

InstaAuto features intelligent queue management. When a Reel goes hyper-viral and exceeds your hourly limit, messages are placed in a safe FIFO queue and systematically dispatched as your rolling 1-hour window resets. No follower is ever lost.

Can I try it for free?

+

Yes! Our Starter Tier is 100% free with 60 DMs/hour, unlimited keyword automation, and click analytics. No credit card is required to sign up.
